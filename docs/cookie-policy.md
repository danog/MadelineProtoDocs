# Cookie Policy

This website does **not** set any cookies, and does not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, analytics, advertising or social media trackers. For this reason it does not show a cookie banner.

## Local storage

The only information this website stores on your device is your choice of light or dark theme, kept in your browser's local storage so that it applies to every page:

| Name | Type | Duration | Purpose |
|------|------|----------|---------|
| `color-theme`, `theme` | local storage, first party | until you clear your browser data | remember the light or dark theme |

This information never leaves your browser and is strictly necessary to provide the theme setting you asked for, so it does not require consent (Art. 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 and Art. 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC).

## Other websites

When you follow a link to another website (GitHub, Telegram, etc.) you leave this website: those pages may use their own cookies, as described in their own cookie policies.

## Managing cookies and local storage

You can see what is stored on your device, block it and delete it at any time from your browser settings, for instance in [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647), [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/clear-cookies-and-site-data-firefox), [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac), [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/windows/manage-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-view-allow-block-delete-and-use-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d), [Brave](https://brave.com/learn/block-cookies/) and [Opera](https://help.opera.com/latest/web-preferences/#cookies). Deleting it only resets your theme choice.
